**Pixel art** is an image drawn one pixel at a time, with a few carefully chosen colors. It comes from early video games, when screens and memory were small, and it stayed as a style because every pixel is a choice made by the artist.

## Why pixels must stay crisp
If you enlarge pixel art with an ordinary tool, the edges blur and the piece loses its character. That is why PixelVault previews are enlarged in whole multiples, with no smoothing.

## How pieces are organized
- **Piece**: a single file, one NFT.
- **Collection**: a themed series by the same artist.
- **Artist**: who created the piece, shown on every page.

## The QR code on the piece
Every delivered piece carries a QR code that opens a public page with the collection, the artist and the owner. The owner stays anonymous: only an alias or a wallet address is shown.
