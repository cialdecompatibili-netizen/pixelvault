PixelVault is under construction. For now the [shop](/en/shop/) has **a single demo piece**, *Prime luci*, a pixel art night with moon and mountains.

## What is ready
- Collections and piece pages in Italian and English.
- Watermarked previews: the original file stays off the site.
- An owner verification page, reached by the QR code.

## What is missing
Payment, NFT minting and automatic file delivery. Until then the demo piece is not for sale.
